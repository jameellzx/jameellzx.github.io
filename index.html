Jarib Academy: filling a skate bootcamp from Instagram

Freelance · Kuwait · Jun–Aug 2026

304parent inquiries
~40students enrolled

Parents' biggest fear is safety. I scripted, filmed and edited 5 ads that hit it head-on. The winner opens with a real student giving the academy 5 stars, then answers the objections: safety gear, pro coaches, "our eyes are always on your kids." It positions Jarib as the only camp worth trying. 2 of the 5 ads won, and we scaled them.

207 KD in ad spend → 2,900 KD in enrollments. About 1 in 8 inquiries became a paying student.

Frequently asked questions

What do you do exactly?

Research, scripts, filming, editing and Meta ads, all in one. You get ads built for your buyer and the campaign that runs them.

Yes. I film and edit, and I can appear on camera. I can also work with your team or with creators.

Yes. Most of my ads are in Kuwaiti Arabic, made for the local market. English works too.

Usually 4–5 angles, so we can test and find a winner fast. Then I make more versions of the winner to scale.

Yes. Good ads need a page that converts. I build premium, easy-to-use sites, plus SEO.
