WASTED DAILY Uploader

WASTED DAILY Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel WASTED DAILY to upload and manage the channel's own videos.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos the owner made, schedule them, add them to the channel's playlists, set their settings (audience, category, language, thumbnail) and read and reply to comments on the channel's own videos. It is used only by the channel owner, on the channel owner's own account. It is not offered to other users.
