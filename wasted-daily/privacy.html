Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

WASTED DAILY Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel WASTED DAILY to manage that channel.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos, change their settings, manage playlists and read and reply to comments on the channel's own videos. It reads only the data it needs for these tasks.

What the app stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app keeps a local log of the owner's uploads (video ids, titles, dates). It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it stores no data about viewers or commenters beyond what is needed to show and answer comments at that moment.

Third parties

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google security settings. Local data is deleted by deleting the app's folder.
